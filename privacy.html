AdventureGo Privacy Policy

AdventureGo is designed to work without requiring an account. Favorites, trip notes and preferences are stored locally on your device using browser storage.

The app may load destination images from third-party image hosting services. Those services may receive normal network information required to deliver the image.

We do not intentionally collect passwords, payment details, contacts or precise location through this app.

For questions about this policy, use the contact method published with the app listing.
